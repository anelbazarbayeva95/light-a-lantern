Light a Lantern · how to

A quiet ritual, in a few gestures

  1. Tap a lantern to kindle its glow and hear its bell — each colour rings a different note.
  2. Kindle all or Dim from the bar below to light or quiet the whole night.
  3. Release to let the lit lanterns drift up and fade away — a small letting-go.
  4. Swipe left or right — or open Collection — to travel between lantern cultures.
  5. Sounds, Ritual and the ☰ menu add ambience, a breathing guide, and a timer.

About

One quiet HTML file

A small ritual gathering lanterns from around the world — the silk lanes of Hội An, Japanese chōchin, Filipino parol stars, Mexican farolitos, and more. Tap a lantern to kindle a warm glow and a soft bell; each colour rings a different note. Swipe or open the Collection to travel between cultures. Layer rain, a temple drone, chimes or crickets, follow the breathing guide, and set a timer that closes with a resonant gong.

Everything — every sound included — is generated live in your browser with the Web Audio API. No dependencies, no assets, no build. It works fully offline.

When you're ready, release the lit lanterns and watch them drift up into the dark.
