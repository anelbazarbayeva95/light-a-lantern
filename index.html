About

One quiet HTML file

A small ritual inspired by the lantern-lit lanes of Hội An, Vietnam. Tap a lantern to kindle a warm glow and a soft bell; each colour rings a different note. Layer rain, a temple drone, chimes or crickets, follow the breathing guide, and set a timer that closes with a resonant gong.

Everything — every sound included — is generated live in your browser with the Web Audio API. No dependencies, no assets, no build. It works fully offline.

When you're ready, release the lit lanterns and watch them drift up into the dark.
